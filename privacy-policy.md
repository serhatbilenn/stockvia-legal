## Information We Collect

Stockvia is designed to keep your home inventory data on your device. Product names, shopping list items, purchase history, notes, and inventory details are stored locally on your device and are not collected by us.

We may collect limited usage and analytics data through Firebase Analytics, such as app interactions, feature usage, device type, app version, and general performance-related information. This data helps us understand how the app is used and improve the app experience.

Payment and subscription information is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Understand app usage
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Maintain and improve Stockvia features
- Support subscription-related functionality
- Respond to support or privacy requests

We do not sell your personal data.

## Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device remains on your device unless you delete it, uninstall the app, or clear app data.
